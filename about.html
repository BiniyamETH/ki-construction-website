Who we are

An established importer, not a shop

KI Construction Materials Importer is an importer, distributor and technical-service company for construction and industrial machinery, established 2006 E.C. in Addis Ababa.

KI Construction Materials Importer was established and registered in 2006 E.C. by Founder and Managing Director Engineer Kedir Ibrahim, who brings 23 years of experience in sales and marketing across the construction and industrial machinery sectors — generating sets, water and solar pumps, solar energy, and electromechanical works.

The head office is in the Samuel Deressa Building in Piassa, Arada Sub-City, Woreda 01, Addis Ababa. KI has built strong market trust around one simple principle: "Quality Comes First; Customer Satisfaction Is Our Passion."

KI is among the leading importers and distributors of construction machinery and spare parts, generating sets, water and solar pumps, pipe fittings and electromechanical products. We are the exclusive Ethiopian agent for Power Trader Co. of South Korea, a manufacturer of hydraulic breakers and accessories. Every product we supply is backed by technical advice, consultancy and after-sales service.

Vision

To become the preferred company in the mechanical, electrical and hardware trading sector, and Ethiopia's leading importer and supplier of high-quality construction materials and machinery — supporting the country's infrastructure development with reliable products and exceptional service.

Mission

To provide durable, efficient machinery and materials at the right time and place, without compromising on quality, for construction, agricultural and industrial projects across Ethiopia.

  • Market leadership — a substantial share of Ethiopia's competitive construction materials market
  • Customer-centric service — premium products with expert consultation and responsive after-sales support
  • Growth — an expanding portfolio, including renewable-energy solutions
