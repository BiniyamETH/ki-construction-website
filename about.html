Who we are

An established importer, not a shop

KI Construction Materials Importer is an importer, distributor and technical-service company for construction and industrial machinery, established 2006 E.C. in Addis Ababa.

KI Construction Materials Importer was established and registered in 2006 E.C. under Founder and Managing Director Mr. Kedir Ibrahim, who brings 22 years of experience in sales and marketing across the construction and industrial machinery sectors — generating sets, water and solar pumps, solar energy, and electro-mechanical works.

The head office is in Addis Ababa, Arada sub city, wereda 01, Samuel Deressa Building, Piassa. KI has built strong and healthy market trust on one simple principle: "Quality is the First, Customer satisfaction is our passion."

KI is among the leading importers and distributors of construction machinery and spare parts, generating sets, water and solar pumps, pipe fittings and electro-mechanical works. We are the exclusive agent of Power Trader Co., South Korea — manufacturer of hydraulic breakers and accessories — and we support every product we supply with technical advice, consultancy and after-sales service.

Vision

To be referred to as the most favourable company in the mechanical, electrical and hardware trading industry, and the leading importer and supplier of high-quality construction materials and machinery in Ethiopia — supporting the country's infrastructure development with reliable products and exceptional service.

Mission

To provide our products at the right time and the right place with zero tolerance for compromise on quality — durable, efficient machinery and materials for Ethiopian construction, agricultural and industrial projects.

  • Market leadership — a substantial share of Ethiopia's competitive construction materials market
  • Customer-centric service — premium products with expert consultation and responsive after-sales support
  • Growth — an expanding portfolio, including renewable-energy solutions
