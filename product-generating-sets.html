Generating sets & power equipment

Diesel and gas generators from 20 kVA to 250 kVA — in silent and open-frame models — with alternators, control panels and turnkey installation.

KI supplies Perkins, Cummins and Güçbir generating sets for factories, commercial buildings, hospitals, hotels, banks and construction sites, plus smaller petrol and diesel gensets for lighter loads. Every set is sized to the load, installed and commissioned by our technical team, with spare parts held locally.

What we carry

  • Perkins silent and open-frame diesel gensets, 20–250 kVA
  • Cummins open diesel generators
  • Güçbir generating sets
  • Small petrol & diesel gensets (Loncin, Blacksmith and similar)
  • Alternators, ATS and control panels
  • Turnkey supply, installation, commissioning & maintenance
